Common Questions

Here are some commonly asked questions about SEO and digital marketing.

SEO, or Search Engine Optimization, is the process of improving a website so that search engines can better understand its content and users can find relevant information more easily through search.

SEO can help improve a website's visibility in search results, make content easier to discover, and attract visitors who are searching for relevant information, products, or services.

On-page SEO focuses on elements within a webpage, such as titles, headings, content, URLs, internal links, images, and other page-level elements that help organize and communicate the topic of a page.

Off-page SEO involves activities outside your website that can contribute to online visibility and authority. These activities can include link building, content promotion, digital PR, and relevant online mentions.

Technical SEO focuses on technical aspects of a website that can affect crawling, indexing, website structure, performance, mobile usability, and how search engines access and understand the site.

Keyword research is the process of identifying words and phrases people use when searching for information online. It can help guide content planning and help businesses understand relevant search topics.

Content optimization can improve the structure, relevance, readability, and usefulness of a webpage. Well-organized content can make information easier for visitors and search engines to understand.

SEO generally requires ongoing work and monitoring. The time required to see changes can vary depending on factors such as the website, competition, content, technical condition, and the type of optimization being performed.

Internal linking means linking one page of a website to another page on the same website. Relevant internal links can help visitors discover related content and create useful connections between pages.
