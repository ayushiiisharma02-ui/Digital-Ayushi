ABOUT DIGITAL AYUSHI

Making SEO Simple, Practical & Useful

Digital Ayushi is an SEO and digital marketing-focused platform created by Ayushi Sharma. The website is designed to share practical information, strategies, and resources related to search engine optimization and online marketing.

Digital Ayushi covers important areas of digital marketing including keyword research, on-page SEO, off-page SEO, technical SEO, content optimization, website analysis, internal linking, and search intent.

The goal is to make SEO easier to understand by presenting useful concepts and practical strategies in a clear and accessible way.
